OUR MISSION

Teach it right, or don't teach it

Army Hub exists so younger players can understand military life, discipline, chain of command, and camaraderie, through role-play grounded in respect for the people who actually serve.

PILLAR 01

Realism

Ranks, drills, and operations modeled on real structure, not Hollywood shortcuts, so every session teaches something true.

PILLAR 02

Respect

We role-play the discipline and duty of service without glorifying violence, honoring the people this game is inspired by.

PILLAR 03

Readiness

Every recruit starts in training. Rank is earned through participation, leadership, and showing up for the squad.

INSIDE THE HUB

Everything a recruit needs

One command center for training, rank, live operations, and the barracks where the community actually lives.

Training Grounds

Boot-camp style onboarding that walks new recruits through basics before they ever join an operation.

Rank Progression

A transparent ladder from Private to Command Staff, earned through drill attendance and leadership.

Live Operations

Scheduled squad-based missions run by staff, briefed and debriefed like the real thing.

Community Barracks

The off-duty space: voice channels, events, and squad chat where the community actually bonds.
